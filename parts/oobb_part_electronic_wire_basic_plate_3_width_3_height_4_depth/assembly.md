# Assemble the basic wire holder

AI-generated CAD images and instructions. Errors possible.

[Illustrated steps and current assembly guides](README.md#assembly)

[Bare plate](basic_wire_plate.png) · [Assembled](basic_wire_assembled.png) · [Exploded](basic_wire_exploded.png)

For one connector, use the printed 3 × 3 plate, one [six-position 2.54 mm header_long section with equal 6.25 mm ends][stock], one [three-way socket housing][housing], three [female crimp sockets][contact], and three wires. The housing and sockets shown are Harwin M20 references for the DuPont-style form.

1. Cut six positions from a 15 mm overall header_long strip such as [LCSC C343639](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C343639.html). With the plate’s front edge facing you, identify the key notch. On the six-position header, count from the end nearest the notch. Push out pins **1, 2 and 6** without shortening the plastic strip. Keep pins **3, 4 and 5**. See the [modified header model][keyed].
2. Pass the three wires up through the rear opening. Crimp one female socket onto each wire and insert the sockets into the back of the three-way housing until they latch.
3. Mate the housing with the three 6.25 mm pins at the back of the header, then lower both into the plate. The six-position strip sits in the crosswise seat; the three equal-length front pins face forward into the keyed recess.
4. Check that the metal pins are recessed, the wires run freely through the rear opening and none are pinched. The colours show three separate conductors and do not prescribe a pinout.
