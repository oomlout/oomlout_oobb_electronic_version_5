# Wire connector presentation build

AI-generated CAD illustrations and instructions. Errors possible. These assemblies have not been physically fit-tested.

The catalogue covers five horizontal header styles (basic, power, motor, motor stepper and I²C) and nine triple-width stacks with 2–10 active rows. Barrel-jack holders use different hardware and are excluded from this header assembly workflow.

The header source generator in OOMP maintains 40 ordinary PCB headers and creates a separate 40-length header_long family. All five prepared horizontal strips and nine prepared vertical strips now derive from header_long, with equal 6.25 mm exposed ends and 15 mm total pin length. Every model has a STEP assembly, separate plastic/contact STL files and a provenance record. Each vertical entry also includes a three-strip STEP arrangement, separate material meshes and its own rendered preview. Snap grooves are cut into the top and bottom of horizontal strips. The vertical models rotate the shared geometry, rather than reconstructing a different insulator.

For a stack of N active rows, use three vertical strips of N+2 positions. Remove pins 1 and N+2, counting bottom to top. The bottom cell spans Z=0–2.54 mm, active rows occupy the next N×2.54 mm, and the top cell sits in the included 2.54 mm top retainer. This arrangement was confirmed by the user. Header centres are X=−1.27, 1.27 and 3.81 mm, Y=−14.40 mm. Each active row uses one three-way housing and three crimp sockets. The retainer is extracted from the production print mesh at X=50 mm and restored to X=0, Z=base depth for assembly; no production geometry is changed.

The front header tips stop inside the front edge: 0.22 mm for horizontal headers and 0.10 mm for vertical headers. The vertical strips are shifted 0.15 mm rearward within the existing seat clearance, with housing fronts at Y=−13.13 mm. Their 2.5 mm bodies leave a 0.02 mm gap before each housing; the equal rear ends enter by approximately 6.23 mm. The models use the HOAUC/LCSC dimensions recorded in [the basic notes](basic_wire_notes.md). This is illustrative fit, not a measured print tolerance. Housing cavities and loose, uncrimped contact wings are simplified; wire bends and jacket diameter are illustrative. Wire colours do not define an electrical pinout.

The two-way housing is produced by `kicad_agents.wire_housing_3d` using the existing `crimp_3d.housing_model` and its drawing-based dimensions. It adds no alternate housing geometry implementation. The three- and four-way housing STEP assets and socket STEP are reused.

`assembly.json` beside each production STL is generated by `render_wire_catalogue.py`. It records quantities, source hashes, images, assembly text and verified local OOMP identities. The stock and prepared header entries represent the same items before/after modification. Their URLs identify the canonical OOMP destinations; newly created records require publication before their remote pages exist.

Rebuild models in the electronic repository, render in this repository, then regenerate navigation and README pages. See [the maintenance commands](../CATALOGUE_BUILD.md). Final sheets are stored beside the corresponding production STLs; hardware sheets live in electronic `parts/<id>/data/` and `parts_source/<id>/`. Raw images, jobs, logs and the local CadQuery environment live under ignored `build/` folders.
